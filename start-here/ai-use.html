AI Use Guide

You'll use AI assistants a lot in this course, and in a few places not at all. This page shows which is which, and how to say what you used.

About 5 minutesApplies to every module
🤖

The short version

  1. AI can help you build and think. In most of this course, using it well is part of the skill.
  2. You answer for what you submit. If it's in your work, you should be able to explain it and say whether it's right.
  3. Say what you used. Disclosing AI use doesn't cost you points.

The reason is the job itself. AI engineers spend their time judging what models produce, and you can't judge work you couldn't do or explain yourself. That's why every HOS starts with AI and ends by hand.

Keep your keys out of it

Never paste an API key or your .env file into an AI chat.

How to say what you used

One or two plain sentences are enough: which tool, what you asked for, and what you did with the answer.

Not useful"I used AI for this assignment."
Useful"Copilot Chat wrote the first version of cancel_appointment. It let patients cancel other patients' slots, so I added the ownership check and a test for it."

How your work is checked

  • No AI detectors. They're unreliable, so your grade won't be based on one.
  • A short follow-up instead. Your instructor may ask you to explain a line of your code, make a small change to it, or say more about a post. If it's your work, that takes a minute or two.
  • If that doesn't resolve it, your instructor follows CityU's Academic Integrity Policy (University Catalog → Student Rights and Responsibilities).

Not sure whether something is allowed? Ask before you submit.
