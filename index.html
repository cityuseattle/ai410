How each module runs: two weeks

Each module centres on a HOS (Hands-On Skill): one build, worth 10% of your grade, done in four stages across the two weeks and submitted once as a GitHub repo link in Brightspace. The other activities are graded separately.

Week 1 · Build & judgeLectureThree short parts, each paired with a quick check.
Week 1Muddiest PointFour-field structured reflection, due before the build. Graded.
Week 1Concept DebateAssigned side → argue the opposite → synthesis. Graded.
Week 1HOS: Create → EvaluateStages 1–2: AI-assisted scaffold, then deliberate testing.
Week 2 · Explain & masterHOS: Analyze → Understand & RefineStages 3–4: explain one mechanism; extend it by hand, no AI.
Week 2Application ChallengeBuild to a spec in a new scenario; defend one decision.
Week 2Knowledge CheckGraded quiz in Brightspace; practice questions in each lesson.

Due dates, submissions, the syllabus and grades are in Brightspace.
