    What this teaches

    • Push is a chain, and it fails silently. Every link has to hold — the app loads, permission is granted, a real device yields a token, the backend stores it, and the job knows which device to notify. Break any one and nothing errors; the notification just never comes.
    • A trace turns "it's slow" or "it's wrong" into a specific span. Retrieval and generation are separate spans on purpose — that's the HOS 4 Analyze question.
    • Faithfulness is measurable. The score here is a word-overlap version of what your hand-written score_faithfulness() does in Understand & Refine.
    • Alerts are a judgment call. Drag the thresholds: too tight and every request pages you (noise); too loose and the hallucination sails through. That's the HOS 4 Application Challenge.
    For your Term Project Write down the one event in your project that deserves a push notification (not five), and the one number you'd put on a dashboard. For the demo, have a real trace open — "here's retrieval, here's generation, here's the faithfulness score" is a strong Week 10 moment.
