What this teaches

  • Chunk size is a trade-off. Set chunk size to 10 and top-k to 1, then ask the return question: the best-scoring chunk mentions returns, but "30 days" landed in the chunk next to it. Push chunk size to 80 instead and it's found — but every chunk now drags ~80 words of mostly irrelevant text into the prompt. Watch the "What the model receives" box grow.
  • The threshold decides whether the model can say "I don't know." Ask the gift-wrapping question: the "Gift cards are non-refundable" chunk scores just high enough to get through, because the two share a word, not a meaning, and the model is tempted to stretch it into a policy. Raise the threshold to 0.20 and nothing gets through, so the model can say so honestly.
  • RAG can break tool calling. Turn on the buggy prompt and ask the math question — the model obeys "only the documents" and refuses to calculate. Evaluate should always include one tool question.
  • Real embeddings compare meaning, not words. This toy only matches shared words (plus a few synonyms). In HOS 2, Gemini's embeddings use task_type="RETRIEVAL_DOCUMENT" at ingest and "RETRIEVAL_QUERY" at question time — the Analyze stage asks you why.
For your Team Project Paste a few paragraphs of your project's documents into the box and ask the three questions your users would ask first. If the key fact doesn't land in the top-k, that's a chunking decision to make before Week 9 — not during the demo.
