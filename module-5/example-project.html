⚠ Illustrative example only. Flashcard Coach, its team and its numbers are made up, and the demo is a scripted HTML model: no real model, database or network calls. Your project doesn't have to look like this. It has to combine all four HOS in an app a real user would want.

The project at a glance

Group of threeAll 4 HOS

The user and the problem. Students in a large intro biology course have a hundred pages of their own lecture notes and no time to turn them into study material. Flashcard Coach is a phone app: upload your notes, ask questions about them, and get flashcards made from your notes, not the internet.

HOSWhat it is in Flashcard CoachLitmus test: why it's really that HOS
HOS 1 · toolsmake_flashcards(topic, count) and schedule_review(date), called by the model when the user asks.The model decides when to make cards. The backend runs the function and sends the cards back.
HOS 2 · RAGNotes the user uploads are chunked, embedded and stored in Chroma. Every answer and every card is built from retrieved chunks.Retrieval over documents users upload, not a hardcoded FAQ. Answers cite the note they came from.
HOS 3 · guardrails & jobsA schema check on tool arguments (count must be 1–20), and note uploads processed by a background worker.Bad arguments get validated and retried, not crashed on. A 40-page upload never blocks the chat.
HOS 4 · mobile, push, tracesAn Expo app, a push notification when uploaded notes are ready, and a Langfuse trace with a faithfulness score on every answer.The team can show a trace for any answer and point at the retrieval span.

The team and who owned what

One owner per area, not per week, as the Roles section suggests. With three people, one person takes two areas.

    Three releases: what was in each

    Phase 1 · 2 · 3

    Flashcard Coach didn't appear in Week 10. Each phase released a working system version and a report, the same as yours will.

    End of Week 3 · Phase 1

    System v0.3

    • Repo with a README, a .gitignore and no keys committed
    • The HOS 1 chat app running in a Codespace, renamed and restyled
    • A one-page proposal with 3 references: the user, the problem, which HOS goes where, and who owns what
    End of Week 7 · Phase 2

    System v0.7

    • Upload notes, ask questions, get cited answers (HOS 1 + HOS 2 end to end)
    • make_flashcards working, but no guardrail yet
    • Uploads still processed inline, so a big upload freezes the chat. Listed honestly as a known issue.
    • A 3–4 page progress report with 12+ references
    End of Week 10 · Phase 3

    System v1.0

    • Guardrail with retry on every tool call; uploads moved to a queue and worker
    • Expo app with push when notes are ready; Langfuse traces with a faithfulness score
    • One-click Codespace launch and a README execution guide with an AI tool disclosure
    • Final report, presentation and demo video
    What they cut, and said so

    Spaced-repetition scheduling (schedule_review only saves a date; it never reminds anyone) and Android push (the team demoed on an iPhone). Both are on the "what didn't make it" slide. That's the Integration Debate in practice: they cut a feature, not observability.

    What makes this a strong example

    Use it as a checklist
    • Every HOS is real, not a token mention. For each one the team can point to a specific part of the app and say why it's that HOS (the litmus test in the first table).
    • One live path shows all four. The demo doesn't tour features one by one. A single story moves through tools, retrieval, the guardrail, the queue, push and the trace.
    • It shows a failure on purpose. The "500 flashcards" request proves the guardrail works, which is more convincing than a demo where nothing goes wrong.
    • The trace is ready before anyone asks. "Show me where HOS 2 is" gets an answer in one click.
    • It's honest about what's missing. The cut features are named, with what the team would do next.
    • Every member is visible. Owners per area, commits from all three, everyone speaks, and the title card names who built what.
